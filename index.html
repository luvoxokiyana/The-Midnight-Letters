A FEW WORDS FROM THE AUTHOR
AN INTRODUCTION

To those who have found their way here,

✳

My dear reader,

For those of you who've found yourselves here, I'll take this moment to introduce myself. I am the person behind these letters, though I would rather let my words tell my story than attempt to do so myself.

The Midnight Letters has no sense of origin and has existed long before I started writing here. While I hold myself to low standards when it comes to intellect, I find it rather important that one expresses their truest thoughts to another soul.

This little corner of the world is intended to be a collection of my thoughts, reflections, observations, and questions. A place where philosophy meets the ordinary, where the smallest moments may hold the greatest meaning, and where we may examine the things we so often take for granted.

I do not intend to present myself as someone with all the answers. Quite the contrary. I believe there is great beauty in questioning, in learning, and in admitting how little we truly know. Perhaps these letters will offer you a new perspective; perhaps they will simply give you something to think about on a quiet evening.

Whatever you may find within these pages, I hope you find something worth carrying with you.

Thank you for taking the time to read my words. I hope that, in some small way, they make your evenings a little less lonely and your thoughts a little more curious.

Yours kindly, M.
